# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

- First release.
- Free look during autorun; hold R2 to steer with the right stick and release it to look around again.
- Left stick lock during autorun, so a stray touch does not cancel it.
- Steer button choice: R2 (default), L2, or any button taught with `/afl learn`.
- Options panel under Options > AddOns, and `/afl` slash commands.
